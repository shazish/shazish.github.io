About

I have a keen eye for things that need tidying up—a slow UI, server calls waiting on each other, or a cache doing more work than it needs to. At Colgate-Palmolive and Hill’s Pet Nutrition, that curiosity has taken me across frontend applications, backend services, and the integrations between them. I like digging into why things happen and finding a simpler way for the system to work.
