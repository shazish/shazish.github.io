About

I have a keen eye for things that need tidying up, and that carries over into how I think about systems. A UI component that’s slow to first render, server calls waiting on each other when they could run in parallel, or a cache being repopulated when the data has barely changed—all of that catches my attention.

I like digging into why those things happen and finding a simpler way for the system to work. At Colgate-Palmolive and Hill’s, that attention has taken me across frontend code, backend services, and the integrations between them.
