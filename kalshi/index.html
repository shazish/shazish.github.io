Kalshi + Polymarket · Research pipeline

Find the evidence.
Then measure the edge.

Pythia looks for prediction markets where the evidence points more strongly to an outcome than the price suggests.

It filters market data, gathers web research, classifies outcomes, checks the supporting evidence, and calculates whether an opportunity remains at the available ask price.

An agent coordinates the workflow. Python handles scanning, validation, calculations, and reporting.

The regular scan workflow

From a market to an opportunity

A high price gets a market considered. Evidence and final checks determine whether it makes the opportunity list.

  1. 01

    Scan for candidates

    Market data · no LLM

    Fetch markets from the platform APIs and apply price, spread, volume, and date filters. Exclude multi-leg combinations and rank eligible candidates for research.

    Output: Candidate snapshots with prices, contract rules, and market-data timestamps.

  2. 02

    Gather current evidence

    Web research

    Research agents work through batches, collecting facts and source URLs for each market. They check recent developments and the actual settlement criteria before any classification is made.

    Quality checks: Validate research, filter irrelevant findings, and retry failed searches. In the agent-led classification path, missing usable research is marked UNCLEAR.

  3. 03

    Classify the outcome

    LLM reasoning

    The classifier weighs supporting and contradicting evidence, selects a side, and records its confidence and what could change the conclusion. Structured output is validated and saved as the run progresses.

    CERTAIN At least 95% model confidenceLIKELY Remaining uncertaintyUNCLEAR Insufficient support

    These are model judgments. A CERTAIN label alone does not qualify a market for an opportunity.

  4. 04

    Challenge the claim. Verify its sources.

    Evidence review

    A second model pass evaluates counterarguments and can downgrade CERTAIN results to LIKELY. The verifier then checks cited article text against the claim and the contract’s rules, including the relevant entity, metric, threshold, and time period.

    Output: Verified, contradicted, or unverifiable evidence, with recorded reasons. Finalization withholds CERTAIN opportunities without current, matching verification.

    Inside verification: checks, examples, and outcomes →

  5. 05

    Refresh the quote, size, and report

    Calculations · no LLM

    Check market availability and quote freshness, then calculate expected edge using the ask price and configured fees. Apply Kelly sizing and cap the position. Finalization writes the report, prints qualifying opportunities, and logs skipped or below-threshold results.

    Output: Excel reports and run artifacts showing the reasoning, verification, quote used, estimated edge, and suggested position size.

What qualifies an opportunity?

The label is only the first gate.

For regular scans, a result must pass structural validation, evidence verification, and market checks before its economics are evaluated.

Evidence

The article must support the claim

Citations must be HTTPS URLs present in that market’s research. A trusted publisher still needs readable article text that supports the claim and matches settlement rules.

Blocked or unknown sources, inaccessible pages, and failed model reviews cannot establish verification. Evidence reviews expire after 24 hours by default.

Market data

The quote must still be usable

Finalization reuses market data for up to five minutes by default. Missing or stale timestamps require a refresh before calculations proceed.

Closed or expired markets, quotes that remain incomplete after refresh, and required refresh failures are excluded. Reports retain the original scan asks and identify the quote used.

3% or 15%

Minimum estimated edge after fees or annualized edge, respectively.

5%

Maximum bankroll allocation per opportunity after Kelly sizing.

Ask price

The entry price used for sizing and edge calculations; the bid is for display.

How to read these numbers: These are configurable defaults in Pythia. Fee calculations use configured platform-specific models; they are estimates, not a live fee schedule. Edge depends on the model’s probability estimate. Annualized edge scales that estimate by time to close; it is not a realized return. Quotes can move after the check.

Choose the search

Two platforms. Four scan modes.

Use k- for Kalshi or pm- for Polymarket. Filters and liquidity settings vary by platform.

Incremental

Look for new or meaningfully changed candidates using cached market state.

k-incremental / pm-incremental

Full

Scan the configured market universe with the primary eligibility filters.

k-full / pm-full

Deep

Look beyond the primary filters. Kalshi’s deep scan adds candidates that pass relaxed filters but fail the primary ones.

k-deep / pm-deep

Anomaly

Look for unusual capital deployment below the usual high-price range, then check for public explanations.

k-anomaly / pm-anomaly
Anomaly scans take a separate path. Quantitative scoring produces STRONG / WATCH / SKIP. An LLM assesses possible public explanations for WATCH and STRONG signals; this is a model check, not a separate web-research batch. STRONG anomalies have their own opportunity route; they do not carry the same CERTAIN evidence-verification gate.

Start a run

From the repository root, ask a tool-enabled agent to run:

python3 pythia-main k-deep

The runner performs the scan and prepares batch files, then prints instructions for the agent to complete research, classification, verification, and finalization. The command by itself does not complete the full workflow. Research and model costs depend on the chosen tools and provider.
